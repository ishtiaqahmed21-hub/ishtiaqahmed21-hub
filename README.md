## What I work on

A two-phase cold plate is only as reliable as its worst channel. I study how manufacturing tolerance and flow instability lower the real dryout limit of parallel-channel cold plates, using reduced-order physics models, CFD and Monte Carlo methods.

## Research

### [two-phase-dryout-knockdown](https://github.com/ishtiaqahmed21-hub/two-phase-dryout-knockdown)
Reduced-order network model of N parallel boiling channels with random width tolerance. At a ±5% tolerance a 50-channel plate loses about 12% of its dryout power, eleven times a single-phase estimate, and a restrictor sized for identical channels lets the loss exceed 75%. Validated against Qu and Mudawar (2003) and Miglani, Weibel and Garimella (2021), with the failure on thermally coupled channels reported. Manuscript in preparation.

`Python` `SciPy` `CoolProp` `Monte Carlo` `two-phase flow`

### [safefactory-bd](https://github.com/ishtiaqahmed21-hub/safefactory-bd)
Open-data screening index for industrial health, safety and environment burden at twelve sites in Bangladesh, built from 1.05 million hourly air-quality records and 2,123 geocoded facilities, with 87 unit tests. I later audited my own index and found nearly collinear inputs, an access distance fixed at zero for seven sites, and a spliced record in the national air-quality archive. A manuscript on the audit and rebuild is in preparation.

`Python` `pandas` `Monte Carlo` `composite indicators`
