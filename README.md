## Featured research

### [two-phase-dryout-knockdown](https://github.com/ishtiaqahmed21-hub/two-phase-dryout-knockdown)
Reduced-order model of imperfection-sensitive dryout in parallel-channel two-phase cold plates. A ±5 % channel-width tolerance lowers the dryout power of a 50-channel plate by about 12 %, roughly 11× a single-phase estimate. Validated against published experiments (Miglani et al. 2021; Qu & Mudawar 2003), with limitations stated openly.
`Python` `SciPy` `CoolProp` `Monte Carlo` `two-phase flow`
